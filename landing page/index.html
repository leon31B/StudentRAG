How does Asymmetric Retrieval prevent semantic drift in study materials?

NVIDIA Nemotron-3.5-Embed-1B utilizes specialized dual projection heads for asymmetric retrieval:

When indexing textbooks or research papers, text chunks are embedded under the passage role. When you ask a question, the input is transformed via the query role into the shared 2048-dimensional semantic manifold. This guarantees that short questions align tightly with comprehensive textbook paragraphs without loss of context.
